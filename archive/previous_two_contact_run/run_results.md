# Completed Fort Severn workshop run

The implemented scripts completed locally and their numerical checks passed; see `outputs/verification/checks.csv` and the per-step logs. This does not establish completion of the full requested model/update suite. **The Li workflow is partial:** only the raw-core spatial update family was applied, separately to depth and carbon. Li area-average, IDW and covariate-regression branches and their corresponding prior updates have not been run. The Sothe display likewise contains a prior plus three updates, not four updates. Inputs and outputs are confined to FS test, with original scripts preserved under `archive/workshop_source`.

- **Field:** all eight cores retained. Stocks integrate measured material only, to the target or sampled bottom. PM-01-A and PM-02-A remain in the 0–30 cm analysis despite ending above 30 cm. Full-precision integration agrees with the independent raw-data check.
- **Script 2:** workshop area-average and tuned IDW maps/totals generated at 1 km for each workshop reporting column. The deeper columns retain measured-to-bottom stocks; these shallow cores do not establish full 50/100 cm inventories.
- **Script 3:** all 119 covariate bands prepared and extracted. Thirteen prespecified models fit all eight cores; spring NDMI excluded because it is missing at some cores. The original workshop AICc rule selected **Mean_only**. Its core holdout RMSE is 4.1563 kg C/m². These holdouts do not include nested model selection.
- **Sothe:** raw-core spatial, area-based and IDW updates completed using the S30 mean and S100-derived SD sensitivity proxy. On the 3,873 valid prior cells, the raw-core update changes the map mean from 31.3878 to 29.9754 kg C/m². Individual-core holdout RMSE is 19.3298 kg C/m² versus 32.8247 for the extracted prior. This does not establish calibrated uncertainty or independent-site performance. FS-04 uses an audited paired-source-pixel fallback 129.72 m away because its containing Sothe pixel is NoData.
- **Li local models:** two explicitly confirmed organic/mineral contacts support a mean-depth model and an exploratory cumulative-carbon curve. All measured curves and two-core holdout outcomes are exported. No Route L contact thresholds, blends or tail extrapolations were used.
- **Li priors:** separate depth and carbon marginal workshop updates, individual-core holdouts and sensitivity runs completed. They are dependent alternative comparisons, not independently fused carbon pools. The local carbon-depth curve at updated depths is available in 14 supported 1 km cells; the remaining depths exceed the local measured curve support and remain NoData in that diagnostic.

The additional data that materially improve Li are confirmed peat contacts for the other six cores and deeper complete peat profiles with bulk density and carbon. `data/field/additional_contact_template.csv` lists the six core IDs. Temporal accumulation rates would also require chronology.
